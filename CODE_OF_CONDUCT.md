# Code of Conduct

InSIS+ adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Please read it in full; the short version:

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's background, experience or language.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language, or publishing anyone's private information,
  including data from someone's InSIS account, without their explicit permission.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when representing the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through
[GitHub's private reporting](../../security/advisories/new) or by e-mail to the address on the maintainers'
GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban contributors,
following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
